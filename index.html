Protect your solar investment.

At Shiva Solar, we do more than maintain solar plants: we help protect your green-energy investment. Our operations, maintenance and management (O&M) services support rooftop, industrial and commercial solar plants from 20 kW to 1 MW. We focus on early fault detection, reliable plant performance and clear reporting, with service scope agreed after a site review.

01 / SOLAR OPERATIONS

Solar operations

Performance oversight helps identify interruptions and review generation trends. Remote monitoring depends on compatible equipment, data access and the agreed service scope.

  • Remote performance monitoring: SCADA tracking, where available and included, reviews generation curves and interruptions.
  • Daily performance analytics: Compare solar conditions with generation data to help identify underperformance.
  • Grid and inverter status: Alerts flag inverter trips, grid issues and net-metering disconnections for prompt follow-up.
02 / SOLAR MAINTENANCE

Solar maintenance

On-site panel care, preventive electrical checks and corrective maintenance, delivered within the agreed scope.

  • Module deep washing: Systematic, chemical-free washing with soft brushing to remove dust, soot and bird droppings.
  • Preventive electrical audits: Inspect accessible AC/DC cables, connectors and junction boxes; tighten connections where appropriate and safe.
  • Thermal scanning: Use thermal imaging to locate hotspots and investigate potential module faults.
  • Corrective and breakdown support: Troubleshoot faults and address wiring, fuse or structure issues as specified in the service agreement.
03 / SOLAR MANAGEMENT

Solar management

Technical documentation, warranty coordination and spares planning support clear maintenance decisions.

  • Generation reporting: Periodic generation and solar-health summaries help review plant performance; reporting frequency is agreed in the service scope.
  • Warranty claim administration: Coordinate with equipment manufacturers on eligible in-warranty claims.
  • Spare parts management: Maintain an on-site inventory plan for essentials such as MC4 connectors, fuses and surge protectors.

Good to know

Solar care questions.

Quick answers to common questions about panel cleaning and ongoing maintenance.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

Cleaning frequency depends on local soiling, weather, site conditions and the system. A site review can help determine an appropriate schedule; no fixed visit frequency is assumed until it is agreed.

What is covered under the Shiva Solar AMC?

Inclusions depend on the selected plan and are confirmed in writing. Options may include panel cleaning, preventive inspections, inverter and electrical checks, generation reporting and agreed breakdown support. Parts, major repairs and response times are included only if explicitly stated.

Do dirty panels permanently damage the solar system?

Heavy soiling or bird droppings can create localized shading and, in some conditions, contribute to overheating (“hotspots”). Hotspots may damage cells over time, so inspection and appropriate cleaning are important.

Which plant sizes and types do you support?

O&M support is offered for rooftop, industrial and commercial solar plants from 20 kW to 1 MW. Availability depends on location, site access and the agreed scope.

Are spare parts and major repairs included in AMC plans?

Not by default. Replacement parts, equipment changes and major civil or structural repairs are included only when specifically listed in the written quotation.

Can you monitor generation remotely?

Remote monitoring can be discussed where compatible equipment, data access and connectivity are available. Availability and reporting frequency are agreed for each site.
